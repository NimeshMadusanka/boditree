Preserve Practice Awaken

Dedicated to the preservation, maintenance, and responsible continuation of Sri Lanka’s heritage.

Bodhi Sevana ( ෙබ ෙසවන) , meaning “Under the Bodhi Tree,” represents a space of cultivation — open to all, without distinction, who seek to learn, refine, and move toward meaningful life. Like the shade of the Bodhi Tree, it offers a setting that is steady and supportive, where knowledge, skill, and practice are developed with care and discipline. At its core, the initiative is grounded in the understanding that heritage must be actively preserved and reinforced through living practice. Through this, it supports the continuity of traditional knowledge systems while creating conditions that are conducive to both cultural and personal development, and to the further enrichment of the Buddha Sāsana.
