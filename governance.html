Governance

Organizational Structure

Board of Directors

Our organization is guided by a committed Board of Directors with expertise in cultural heritage, education, and community engagement. They oversee strategic planning and ensure alignment with our mission.

Executive Team

The Executive Team manages day-to-day operations and coordinates programs across research, documentation, and community outreach. They work in close collaboration with partner organizations and institutions.

Advisory Council

Our Advisory Council brings diverse perspectives from scholars, practitioners, and community leaders. They provide guidance on technical matters and ensure our work aligns with authentic cultural practices.

Accountability & Transparency

As a non-profit organization, we are committed to transparent operations and regular reporting. Our finances are audited annually, and detailed program reports are made available to stakeholders and the public.
